# Sudhanshu Pandey

I work at the intersection of atmospheric science, satellite remote sensing,
and machine learning. Most of my research is on **methane and CO₂**:
detecting plumes from satellite imagery, quantifying their emission rates,
and folding those observations into atmospheric-inversion systems to refine
bottom-up source estimates. I'm increasingly interested in using ML
emulators to scale those inversions to global, near-real-time use — and in
where ML helps versus where physics-based assimilation still wins.

This is a small notebook for paper sketches, side experiments, and the
occasional weekend AI demo.
